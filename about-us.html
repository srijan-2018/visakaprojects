|| About Us ! ||

Experience A Better Way To Build.

“Visaka Projects” is a partnership company, established in year 2017. An originator of company came to be known under the individual name of Vipin Chandra Yadav (M-tech in CE&M having title of “Chartered Engineer” with 12 years’ Experience), Sandip Ghosh (Diploma in Civil with 19 years’ Experience), and Kamal Jana (Diploma in Civil with 18 years’ Experience). Since then, the Company has continued to deliver the best value in Engineering, construction and consultancy services by placing expert Designer/construction Engineers/Professionals/Technician in all their projects.
“Visaka Projects” has always ranked among the respectable firms in the industry, and its partners has successfully completed some of the most challenging and iconic projects in the Haldia.

Meet With Our Mission

To procure projects at competitive pricing, provide safe working conditions and deliver quality work within reasonable time frame.

What Is Our Vision?

Our Vision is to create and maintain an organization of extremely expert construction professionals that serve our Clients’ needs with honesty and integrity
